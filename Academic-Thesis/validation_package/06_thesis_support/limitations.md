# Project Limitations for Thesis

The following limitations should be stated clearly to avoid overclaiming.

1. The system does not provide official disease diagnosis.
2. The system does not infer NPK nutrient status.
3. The confirmed architecture should not claim pH support unless the hardware and calibration are documented.
4. Raw soil EC must not be converted into saturated-paste ECe without field calibration.
5. The system does not compute ET because wind speed and solar radiation are not currently measured.
6. Yield prediction is not supported from sensor readings alone.
7. Manual agronomic events depend on operator accuracy and event confidence.
8. Soil moisture thresholds require field calibration before absolute water-stress claims.
9. Upload time is transfer metadata and must not be interpreted as measurement time.
10. Generated validation figures are derived from exported data and are not direct screenshots of a live deployed UI.
