# Master Thesis Plan — Three-Chapter Supervisor Structure

## Front matter

- Acknowledgements
- Abstract
- Résumé
- ملخص
- List of Figures
- List of Tables
- List of Abbreviations

## General Introduction

Purpose: present the general motivation, problem, objectives, contributions, and thesis organization. Do not put detailed wiring, database tables, dashboard implementation details, or results here.

Must cover:

- Smart agriculture and the need for reliable field data.
- Saharan/agricultural constraints: power, Internet, field accessibility, manual monitoring limitations.
- Alfalfa as the chosen crop: forage importance, demand, fast regrowth, local data gap.
- Problem statement: lack of crop-specific, offline-first, reliable IoT data collection and analytics for alfalfa field monitoring.
- Objectives: design and implement IoT data collection, local storage, upload, backend, dashboard, deterministic analytics, and interpretation support.
- Contributions: IoT architecture, firmware modes, RTC policy, recovery, dashboard, deterministic analytics, Processed Data Viewer, AI interpretation boundary.
- Thesis organization: exactly three chapters.

## Chapter 1 — Background and Theoretical Foundations

Purpose: build the theoretical base. Use references heavily. Do not compare previous works deeply here; reserve that for Chapter 2.

## Chapter 2 — Related Works and Comparative Analysis

Purpose: analyze previous works thematically, identify limitations, and show how the proposed project improves them. This is the most citation-sensitive chapter and must avoid plagiarism by comparing function, not copying source prose.

## Chapter 3 — System Design, Implementation, and Evaluation

Purpose: explain the implemented system step by step: methodology, architecture, hardware, firmware modes, communication, data storage, backend, dashboard, analytics, AI, field deployment, validation, limitations.

## General Conclusion

Purpose: summarize what was achieved, restate contribution, acknowledge limitations, and suggest future work.
