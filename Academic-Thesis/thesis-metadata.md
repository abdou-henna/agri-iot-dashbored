## Scope Statement

This thesis presents the design, implementation, and validation of a smart farm monitoring platform for alfalfa cultivation. The system integrates distributed ESP32-based sensing nodes, LoRa communication, RTC-based timestamping, local SD-card persistence, a Node.js/Express backend, PostgreSQL storage, and a React dashboard. The platform combines automatic sensor measurements with manually recorded agronomic events such as irrigation, fertilization, field notes, and cutting-related context. Its analytics layer is deterministic and reliability-aware, while AI is restricted to interpretation and does not compute authoritative measurements or agronomic metrics.
